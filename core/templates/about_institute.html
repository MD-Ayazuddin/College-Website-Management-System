About Institute

Know more about Tauheed Institute of Technology

Welcome to TIIT (Tauheed Institute of Information Technology), an emerging IT-Institution for better Education. The Tauheed Institute of Information Technology, a Knowledge Resource Center (College) of Maulana Mazharul Haque Arabic and Persian University, Patna, popularly known as TIIT, was established in 2008 with a vision to contribute to the IT world by focusing on better education and guidance.

We are committed to provide high quality academic and non academic facilities to create such a stimulating and effective environment where students can grow holistically and which enable them to face the new challenges of IT world and make them capable to produce best results in every circumstances.
