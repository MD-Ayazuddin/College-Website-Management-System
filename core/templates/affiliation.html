About The University:- Maulana Mazaharul Haque Arabic and Persian University has been established under the provisions of the Bihar State University Act 1976 (as amended up to date) with effect from April 10, 1998 by the Government of Bihar. The Department of Human Resource Development, Govt. of Bihar has notified its Functions Vide memo no. He 1551 dated 24.07.2008 stating that “To confer or grant degree, diploma, certificate and other academic distinctions in Arabic or Persian or in such other branches of learning, including professional education, as the University may consider fit”. Although the University was established in 1998 yet it had remained non functional till January 2008. The University has started its First Academic Session from July 2008 by admitting students to nine job Oriented vocational courses through the Knowledge Resource Center Network established in the different districts of Bihar.
