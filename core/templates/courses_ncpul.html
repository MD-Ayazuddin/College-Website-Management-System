CABA-MDTP

1 Year Diploma

Diploma in Computer Applications, Business Accounting & Multilingual DTP (CABA-MDTP)

This CABA-MDTP one-year diploma course has been designed for transformation of Urdu speakers into productive human resources and making them part of the employable technological work force in the emerging IT scenario and penetration of computer education to grass root level. Taking base as the DOEACC ‘O’ Level course contents, the contents have been enriched with

Programming Techniques
Database Applications
Tally & Accounting
Web Designing Tools
CorelDraw & Photoshop
Urdu & Hindi DTP (InPage)

The course in Accounting Package (Tally), Urdu and DTP software is taught in addition to DOEACC ‘O’ level course. CABA-MDTP course is a modular course.

The course contents of CABA-MDTP include those of DOEACC ‘O’ level and CCC of DOEACC in addition to Tally and Multilingual DTP.

The contents have been designed keeping in view that there will be number of career options possible for an individual after successfully completing the course.

In addition to CABA-MDTP diploma the student must appear in the DOCEACC CCC examination after having gone training in M1 and M2 modules. However after 1st semester of CABA-MDTP diploma examination, the student can appear for the modules for which he/she has undergone training at the NCPUL Centre. Thereby the student who has undergone training in CABA-MDTP diploma can get certificates of three courses viz CABA-MDTP, DOEACC CCC and DOEACC ‘O’ level after successfully passing the respective examinations. For ‘O’ level Certificate, the centers should have accreditation by DOEACC.

Diploma in Urdu

1 Year Diploma

The council has launched one year Diploma course in Urdu through English and Hindi medium. Admission in this course is compulsory and passing in one year diploma course in Urdu will be a precondition for award of Diploma in Computer Applications, Business Accounting and Multilingual DTP.
