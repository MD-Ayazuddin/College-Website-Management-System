Bachelor in Computer Application (BCA)

1. Objective

To develop and equip the students with essential knowledge and skill in order to develop expertise in the key areas of computing service and information technology. Students will be familiarized with the concepts of computer science and its various applications, it is a training based programme which will develop practical skill to enable them to become solution provider.

2. Eligibility

10+2 or its equivalent examination passed candidates from a recognized board including Madarsa Education Board, Bihar, in Arts/Science/Commerce securing 45 marks in the aggregate, preference will be given to stude3nts having Mathematics and English as subjects in +2 examination.

3. Duration

Three years degree programme consisting of three academic years each divided into two semesters of six months duration.

4. Fee Structure

Rs. 8500/- each year, in the first year of admission students will pay Rs. 1000/- as the Development Fund contribution,

Rs. 200/- as Registration and Misc. charge and 1000/- ,

Examination Fees Rs. 500/- Tarang & Eklavya Rs. 10/- for each year of Examination.

  • The course will be conducted on self finance basis.
  • SC/ST candidates will be allowed 20% concession in tuition fee.
  • Besides the above noted fees and charges, each candidate appearing in the University examination will be required to pay the prescribed examination fee to the University for each examination.
  • The fee may be revised by the University in changing circumstances.
  • Preference in admission will be given to those passing out form Madrsa System of Education.

5. Relevant Information

  1. Reservation: Reservation of seats as per Bihar Government Rules.
  2. Medium of instruction: Hindi/English/Urdu
  3. Seat Limit: The total number of seats 60.
  4. Training: Training facility to be provided as per the requirement of the course by the KRCs.
  5. Dress Code & Discipline: The students must observe dress code and follow rules as prescribed by the University.
